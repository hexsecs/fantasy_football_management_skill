# Weekly cadence

The standing schedule the system runs on. Times below are the proven defaults in the owner's timezone; the owner's actual times live in the `schedule` section of their owner config. Adapt the clock times at onboarding, but keep the relationship between each check and the kickoff or waiver event it serves.

## Every morning: health check (about 8:40 AM)

- Roster health for every team: injury designations, practice participation, and the latest news for starters and key bench players.
- Matchup context: the week's opponent, the live projected score, and the two or three swing players most likely to decide it.
- Free-agent landscape: notable adds and drops league-wide and emerging targets, evaluated under the waiver framework.
- Lock deadlines: any decision needed before the next kickoff, with the exact lock time.
- Strategy refinement: the week-long plan updated (stash candidates, drop candidates, next week's matchup plays).
- Deliver one short brief per the reporting rules in SKILL.md. Execute for full-auto leagues, recommend for the rest.

## Tuesday morning: recap and grading (about 7:40 AM)

- Final scores against projections, the two or three deciding performances, record and standing movement.
- Grade the week's calls under the rubric in `references/decision-frameworks.md`, including bench-beat-starter cases.
- Refresh the all-32 defense board for the coming week.
- Name the first waiver targets for Wednesday so the evening preview starts from evidence, not a cold scan.

## Tuesday evening: waiver preview (about 6:40 PM)

- For recommendations-only leagues, present the ranked claim list with adds, drops, confidence numbers, and the cost of each claim under that league's mechanics.
- Post it early enough that the owner has the evening to decide before overnight processing.
- For full-auto leagues, this is the internal planning pass for the Wednesday execution.

## Wednesday morning: waiver pass (about 7:40 AM)

- Full-auto leagues: file or verify the claims before the processing window, then confirm outcomes once they process and re-verify the roster live.
- Recommendations-only leagues: report which of the owner's claims landed, which failed, and what the fallback is for each failure (usually a free-agent add once waivers clear).
- Reflect the results in the same day's health check. A claim that failed on priority is information about the league's appetite, and it feeds the next preview.

## Thursday: pre-lock check before Thursday night football (about 7:00 PM for an 8:15 PM kickoff)

- Verify the Thursday inactives when they publish, about 90 minutes before kickoff, for every rostered player in the game on any team.
- A questionable starter in a Thursday game with no bench cover is the single highest-risk slot in the system. Prepare the full contingency in advance: the add, the drop, and the exact slot changes, so the window between inactives and lock is spent executing.
- Bring any contingency move on a recommendations-only league to the owner inside that window, with the deadline stated. A player freezes in his slot at kickoff even if inactive, and some drops become impossible once the dropped player's own game has started.
- Confirm the full-auto leagues' Thursday starters are active, and execute the prepared contingency yourself if one is needed.

## Sunday morning: lineup check (about 10:40 AM) and inactives sweep (about 11:40 AM)

- The lineup check verifies every starter is healthy and expected to play, and catches early kickoffs (international games can lock at 9:30 AM). A check timed for the 1 PM slate does not cover a 9:30 AM lock, so weeks with early games move this check earlier.
- The inactives sweep, about 90 minutes before the 1 PM kickoffs, catches the late outs the first check cannot see. Re-verify any questionable starter against the official inactive list.
- Before any swap instruction, verify the player's current starting slot on the live roster and name the exact swap (who leaves the slot, who enters it, and what happens at the slot he vacates).
- Watch the bench-lock trap: a bench player in a 1 PM game cannot replace a starter whose game is later. If the starter is doubtful, the swap decision expires at the bench player's lock.

## Sunday afternoon and Monday: late pre-lock checks

- A short check before the late Sunday window covers questionable starters in the 4 PM and Sunday night games, and any last flex decision between a late player and a Monday player.
- A Monday evening check before Monday night football is the last call for Monday starters and their cover decisions. After this lock there is no recourse, so a doubtful Monday starter with a healthy Sunday alternative should usually have been resolved on Sunday.
- Daylight saving transitions shift the late windows by an hour in some weeks. Verify the week's actual kickoff times from the schedule, not from habit.

## Bye-week and season planning (continuous)

- Track the bye calendar two weeks out. A starter's bye-week hole is visible a fortnight ahead, and the cover is cheaper to acquire before the week arrives.
- Keep the running back stable stocked ahead of the byes, not during them.
- Late in the season, shift stash value toward playoff-week schedules and handcuffs for the owner's own starters.
